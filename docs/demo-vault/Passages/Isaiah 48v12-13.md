> “I am He; I am the first, and I am the last. Surely My own hand founded the earth.” (Isa 48:12–13, BSB)

- Here the title goes with creation: the hand that founded the earth and spread out the heavens.
- Compare Isa 41:4 and Isa 44:6.
- Rev 1:17 has “the First and the Last” on the lips of the risen Jesus.
