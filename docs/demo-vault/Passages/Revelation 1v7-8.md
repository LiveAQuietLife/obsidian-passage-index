> “Behold, He is coming with the clouds, and every eye will see Him—even those who pierced Him. And all the tribes of the earth will mourn because of Him.” (Rev 1:7, BSB)

- Two Old Testament texts in one verse: Dan 7:13 (coming with the clouds) and Zech 12:10 (those who pierced him; the mourning).
- Zechariah has the families of Jerusalem mourning; here it is “all the tribes of the earth”. Matt 24:30 makes the same change.
- John 19:37 uses Zech 12:10 for the cross; here it looks ahead.
- 1:8 “I am the Alpha and the Omega” is the Lord God speaking. Rev 21:6 and Rev 22:13 repeat it; in 22:13 it is joined to “the First and the Last” (Rev 1:17).
