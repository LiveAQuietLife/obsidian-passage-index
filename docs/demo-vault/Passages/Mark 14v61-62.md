> “I am,” said Jesus, “and you will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power and coming with the clouds of heaven.” (Mark 14:62, BSB)

- The high priest asks whether he is the Christ, the Son of the Blessed One (14:61).
- The answer joins two texts: Ps 110:1 (sitting at the right hand) and Dan 7:13 (coming with the clouds).
- Matt 26:64 adds “from now on”.
