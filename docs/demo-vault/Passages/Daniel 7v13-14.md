> “I saw One like the Son of Man coming with the clouds of heaven. He approached the Ancient of Days and was led into His presence.” (Dan 7:13, BSB)

- The figure comes *to* the Ancient of Days (7:9), not down to earth.
- He is given dominion, glory and kingship, and every nation serves him (7:14).
- Later texts take up “coming with the clouds”: Mark 14:62, Matt 24:30, Rev 1:7.
- “One like the Son of Man” comes back in Rev 1:13 and Rev 14:14.

## Intertexts

- Mark 14:62 (alluded to by) — Jesus before the high priest
- Rev 1:7 (alluded to by)
