> “And, as another Scripture says: ‘They will look on the One they have pierced.’” (John 19:37, BSB)

- John gives two Scriptures for what happened at the cross: no bone broken (19:36) and the piercing (19:37).
- 19:37 is the first half of Zech 12:10. The mourning is left out here.
- Rev 1:7 has the same “pierced” wording, looking ahead to his coming.

## Intertexts
