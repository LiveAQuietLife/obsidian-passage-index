> “Do not be afraid. I am the First and the Last, the Living One. I was dead, and behold, now I am alive forever and ever!” (Rev 1:17–18, BSB)

- John falls like a dead man, as Daniel does (Dan 10:9–10). A hand touches him and he is told not to be afraid.
- Isaiah's title for the LORD (Isa 41:4; 44:6; 48:12) is spoken by the one who was dead and lives.
- The letter to Smyrna opens with these words (Rev 2:8).
- He holds “the keys of Death and of Hades”.

## Intertexts

- Isa 44:6 (alludes to)
- Isa 48:12 (alludes to)
- Dan 10:9 (echoes)
