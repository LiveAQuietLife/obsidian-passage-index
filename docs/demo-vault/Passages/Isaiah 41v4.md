> “I, the LORD—the first and the last—I am He.” (Isa 41:4, BSB)

- The setting is a trial: the LORD against the nations and their idols (41:1–7, 21–29). Who called the generations from the beginning?
- The first of three “first and last” sayings: Isa 41:4, 44:6, 48:12.
- “I am He” again in Isa 43:10 and Isa 48:12.
- Revelation gives the title to the risen Jesus: Rev 1:17; 2:8; 22:13.
