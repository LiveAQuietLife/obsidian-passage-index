> “I am the Alpha and the Omega, the First and the Last, the Beginning and the End.” (Rev 22:13, BSB)

- Three pairs together. “The Alpha and the Omega” is the Lord God's title in Rev 1:8 and Rev 21:6; “the First and the Last” is the risen Jesus' in Rev 1:17 and Rev 2:8.
- Behind “the First and the Last”: Isa 41:4; 44:6; 48:12.
- 22:12 “My reward is with Me” sounds like Isa 40:10.
