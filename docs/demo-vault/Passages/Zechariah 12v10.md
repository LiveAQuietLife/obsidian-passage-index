> “They will look on Me, the One they have pierced. They will mourn for Him as one mourns for an only child.” (Zech 12:10, BSB)

- The LORD pours out “a spirit of grace and prayer”, and the mourning follows.
- The mourning is for “an only child” and “a firstborn son”, and goes family by family (12:12–14).
- John 19:37 quotes the first half at the cross.
- Rev 1:7 joins it to Dan 7:13: the one coming with the clouds is the one they pierced.
- Matt 24:30 also has “all the tribes of the earth will mourn” next to the clouds.
