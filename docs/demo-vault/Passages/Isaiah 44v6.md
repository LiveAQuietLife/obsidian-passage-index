> “I am the first and I am the last, and there is no God but Me.” (Isa 44:6, BSB)

- Three titles first: King and Redeemer of Israel, the LORD of Hosts.
- Here the point of “first and last” is that there is no rival god (44:8; Isa 45:5).
- Isa 41:4 and Isa 48:12 say the same.
- Rev 22:13 gives the words to Jesus.
