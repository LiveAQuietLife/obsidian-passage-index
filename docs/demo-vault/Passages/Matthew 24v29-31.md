> “All the tribes of the earth will mourn. They will see the Son of Man coming on the clouds of heaven with power and great glory.” (Matt 24:30, BSB)

- 24:30 puts Zech 12:10 (the tribes mourn) beside Dan 7:13 (coming on the clouds), as Rev 1:7 does.
- In Zechariah the mourners are the families of Jerusalem; here they are “all the tribes of the earth”.
- The darkened sun and falling stars (24:29) sound like Isa 13:10.
- Compare Mark 13:26 and Luke 21:27.

## Intertexts

- Dan 7:13 (alludes to)
- Zech 12:10 (alludes to)
- Isa 13:10 (echoes)
