> “These are the words of the First and the Last, who died and returned to life.” (Rev 2:8, BSB)

- Each letter opens with words from the vision in chapter 1. Smyrna's come from Rev 1:17–18.
- The church faces prison and death (2:10); the one speaking has died and come back to life.
- “Be faithful even unto death, and I will give you the crown of life” (2:10).
- “The second death” (2:11) comes back in Rev 20:6, 14 and Rev 21:8.
