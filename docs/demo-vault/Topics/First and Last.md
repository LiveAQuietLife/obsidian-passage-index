# The First and the Last

The LORD's title in Isaiah, given to the risen Jesus in Revelation.

## In Isaiah

- [[Isaiah 41v4|Isa 41:4]]: in the trial of the nations' gods.
- [[Isaiah 44v6|Isa 44:6]]: “there is no God but Me”.
- [[Isaiah 48v12-13|Isa 48:12]]: with the creation of the earth.

## In Revelation

- [[Revelation 1v17-18|Rev 1:17–18]]: “I was dead, and behold, now I am alive”.
- [[Revelation 2v8-11|Rev 2:8]]: the letter to Smyrna.

## Alpha and Omega

A close title: Rev 1:8; 21:6; 22:13.
