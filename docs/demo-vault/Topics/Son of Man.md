# Son of Man

“One like the Son of Man” in Daniel, taken up in the Gospels and Revelation.

- [[Daniel 7v13-14|Dan 7:13–14]]: comes with the clouds to the Ancient of Days and is given the kingdom.
- [[Mark 14v61-62|Mark 14:62]]: Jesus' answer at his trial, with Ps 110:1.
- [[Matthew 24v29-31|Matt 24:30]]: coming on the clouds, the tribes mourning.
- [[Revelation 1v7-8|Rev 1:7]]: coming with the clouds.
- Rev 1:13: “One like the Son of Man” among the lampstands.
- Rev 14:14: seated on a white cloud.
