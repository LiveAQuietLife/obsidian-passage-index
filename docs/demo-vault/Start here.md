# Start here

A small vault for trying Passage Index. The notes follow two threads through the Bible:

- **Coming with the clouds:** from Daniel and Zechariah to the Gospels and Revelation.
- **The first and the last:** a title of the LORD in Isaiah, given to the risen Jesus in Revelation.

Open the Passage Index pane (the ribbon icon, or the command palette: *Passage Index: Open pane*), then open any note in `Passages`. The pane shows the other notes that touch the same verses.

The notes are short study notes, not commentary. Quotations are from the Berean Standard Bible (BSB), which is in the public domain.
